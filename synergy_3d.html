Our Story

Built on precision.
Driven by innovation.

01

How We Started

In November 2014 when Synergy 3D was established, we only produced restorations that came out of a milling unit. While analog labs were struggling to integrate digital technology, we were already developing and improving the digital workflow. Today we are proud to say 98% of our restoratives are produced digitally.

02

How We Operate

It all starts with the technician. Each technician employed by Synergy 3D has been selectively chosen based on their experience, expertise and knowledge. Not only are they constantly willing to learn, they are also willing to educate. If your office has questions or wants training on scanners, we are here to help.

03

How We Sustain

Without your consistent loyalty we would not be where we are today. Thank you for trusting your business in us. We are always looking to improve and love receiving customer feedback. Please take the time to leave us a review as we would greatly appreciate it!

3-Step Setup for iTero

1

Open Rx form in MyiTero portal

Log in to your MyiTero account and navigate to the New Prescription form from the dashboard.

2

Add Synergy 3D as recipient lab

Search for "Synergy 3D" in the lab directory and add us as your preferred lab partner.

3

Send the case — we receive it instantly

Submit your scan directly from MyiTero. Our floor receives it within seconds and begins processing same day.

File Requirements

.STL or .DCM exported via MyiTero cloud. No manual file download required when sending directly through the portal.

3-Step Setup for 3Shape

1

Open 3Shape Communicate

Launch 3Shape Communicate from your TRIOS scanner or desktop and navigate to New Order.

2

Search and connect Synergy 3D

Use the lab search to find "Synergy 3D" and send a connection request. We approve within 1 business hour.

3

Submit order with scan attached

Complete the Rx form, attach your TRIOS scan, and submit. We receive the full case package instantly.

File Requirements

.3OXZ or exported .STL via 3Shape Communicate. Bite scan and antagonist required for crown and bridge cases.

3-Step Setup for Medit

1

Log in to Medit Link

Access Medit Link from your i700 or i900 scanner interface or at link.meditcloud.com.

2

Add Synergy 3D as a lab partner

Go to Partners → Labs and search for Synergy 3D. Send a partnership request — we respond within the hour.

3

Send the completed order

Attach your scan, complete the prescription form, and submit. Files arrive on our floor immediately.

File Requirements

.STL or .OBJ via Medit Link cloud transfer. Include upper, lower, and bite scans. No size limit for cloud transfers.

3-Step Setup for Carestream

1

Export scan from CS 3700 / 3800

Complete your scan and use the "Export" function to save the files in .STL or .DCM format to your computer.

2

Complete the Synergy 3D Rx form

Download and fill out our digital Rx form. Attach the exported scan files directly to the form.

3

Upload via our portal

Log in to your Synergy 3D portal and upload the Rx form and scan files. We confirm receipt within 30 seconds.

File Requirements

.STL or .DCM format exported from CS 3700 / 3800. Include all arches and bite registration files.
